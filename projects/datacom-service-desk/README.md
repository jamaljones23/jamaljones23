## Project overview

I completed the Datacom Service Desk Job Simulation on Forage to practice managing support requests and communicating during a network outage. The exercise connected technical troubleshooting with the service desk responsibilities of prioritizing work, escalating an issue, and keeping users informed.

## Scenario

The exercise involved incoming client support tickets and a network outage affecting connectivity. In the supplied scenario, the network team identified an infrastructure failure in the main data center. The scenario included an estimated restoration window of 24 hours while engineers continued working on a fix and monitoring stability.

That restoration window was information supplied by the exercise, rather than a measured outage duration from a production system I operated.

## Work I practiced

| Activity | My focus |
| --- | --- |
| Categorizing and logging tickets | Organizing reported problems and recording information useful for follow-up |
| Assessing impact and urgency | Considering how an issue affected users, departments, and business operations |
| Prioritizing requests | Deciding which work needed attention first based on the situation |
| Escalating the outage | Recognizing when an issue required the network team's investigation |
| Communicating with users | Drafting updates that explained the reported cause, expected restoration window, and ongoing work |

## Outcome

I completed the simulation and practiced a structured approach to ticket triage, escalation, and user follow-up. The exercise helped me connect incident management concepts with clear customer communication.

This write-up summarizes my completed simulation work. Original ticket submissions and a completion certificate are not attached to this repository.

## Connection to cybersecurity

Availability is part of the confidentiality, integrity, and availability triad. Service desk triage helps identify the scope of a disruption and route useful information to the appropriate team. A connectivity outage by itself does not establish that a cyberattack occurred.

## What I learned

- Technical details are most useful when they help someone make a decision or take the next step.
- The number of affected users and the effect on business operations matter when prioritizing requests.
- An estimated restoration time should be communicated as an estimate.
- Documenting and escalating an issue are meaningful parts of incident handling.
