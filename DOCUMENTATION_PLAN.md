## Structure for the next write-up

Use the following questions to organize an actual lab:

1. What was the goal, and what environment did I use?
2. What did I personally do?
3. What commands, settings, or procedures did I use?
4. What did I expect to happen?
5. What happened, and what evidence shows it?
6. What did I troubleshoot or change?
7. What is the final result, including any unresolved limitation?
8. What did I learn?

Before attaching a screenshot, log, or configuration, remove passwords, tokens, personal data, and information from systems that cannot be shared publicly.
